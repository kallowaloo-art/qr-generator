Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Signal QR Code Generator ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise handles your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as phone numbers you enter to generate QR codes. All data processing is done locally in your browser.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely to generate QR codes for Signal contacts. We do not store, transmit, or retain any of the information you provide.

4. Data Storage

All QR code generation happens in your browser. No data is sent to our servers or third parties.

5. Cookies

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing habits.

6. Third-Party Services

This service may use third-party libraries (such as QRCode.js) under their respective licenses. These libraries do not collect personal information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@signal-qr.com.
